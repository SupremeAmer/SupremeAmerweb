Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about investing with SupremeAmer.

What is the minimum investment?

Minimum investments vary by project, typically starting at $5,000 for Seed Sponsor tier. Each project listing displays its specific minimum.

How are projects vetted?

Our investment committee conducts rigorous due diligence including financial audits, team background checks, market analysis, and technical feasibility reviews.

When do I see returns?

Return timelines vary by project, typically 18-36 months. Preferred returns begin accruing immediately upon investment confirmation.

Can I invest from outside the US?

Yes, we accept international investors subject to applicable securities regulations in your jurisdiction. Contact us for details.
